The Shrinking Song: Duration Across Decades

Why are modern songs getting shorter? In the 1960s, 7-inch vinyl records limited songs to under 3 minutes. The CD revolution in the 1980s and 1990s unlocked epic 5-minute anthems. But since 2015, streaming royalty models and short-form video have pushed hit songs down to historic lows. Crucially, streaming research shows that people typically decide whether to skip a song within the first 30 seconds (0.5 minutes)—the exact cutoff needed to earn a royalty.

The Rise of Danceability

Spotify’s danceability metric scores how suitable a track is for dancing based on tempo, rhythm stability, beat strength, and overall regularity. As drum machines, club synthesizers, and hip-hop 808s took over top-40 radio, the danceability of Billboard hits increased by over 25%.
