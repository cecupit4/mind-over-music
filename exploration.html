Genres & Societal Changes

How popular music mirrors cultural, political, technological, and demographic shifts throughout history

Historical and societal analysis adapted from How Music Genres Reflect Societal Changes Throughout History (Fabulist), examining music as a cultural barometer for political movements, economic conditions, technological revolutions, and demographic shifts.

1. Historical Era Genre Distribution

Comparing genre market share across each historical era. Segments show the percentage each genre contributed to that era’s Billboard Top 100 hits.

2. Historical Milestone Impact Scatterplot

Each point plots the immediate market share shift (+/– percentage points) caused by a major historical event. Points in the upper half (above 0%) gained popularity; points in the lower half declined. Labels show the impacted genre and percentage change.

3. Historical Events Data & Genre Trajectory (1958 – 2024)

Diamond data markers plot actual Billboard historical event data points directly on each genre’s timeline. Hover over any diamond to see the event metrics, societal movement, and iconic Billboard hit song from that year.
