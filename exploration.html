Genres & Societal Changes

How popular music mirrors cultural, political, technological, and demographic shifts throughout history

Historical and societal analysis adapted from Impact of Historical Events on Music (StudyGuides / MUSC 126A Honors: Music in History and Culture), exploring how wars, civil rights movements, revolutions, and digital technology have actively shaped popular music genres throughout history.

1. Historical Era Genre Distribution

Comparing genre market share across the major historical epochs identified in the StudyGuides curriculum. Segments show the percentage each genre contributed to that era’s Billboard Top 100 hits.

2. Historical Event Impact: Market Share Shifts

Measuring the net percentage-point shift in genre market shares immediately following major historical events (comparing the 2-year post-event window to the 2-year pre-event baseline). Bars extending right show market share gains; bars extending left show declines.

3. Historical Events Data & Genre Trajectory (1958 – 2024)

Diamond markers plot actual Billboard historical event data points directly on each genre’s timeline. Hover over any diamond to see the event metrics, curriculum movement from StudyGuides, and iconic Billboard hit song from that year.
